About
The Band

after losing full hearing in my ear due to an acoustic neuroma, I took on the pseudonym, taqed, to create music and tell stories I previously lacked the courage to share.

while I am racing against time and inevitable deafness, I am pausing to celebrate the joy of hearing and the magic of sound.

taqed makes music that gets under your skin — lyrics that look you straight in the eye.

the name is a provocation. the music is the result

For bookings and press: booking@taqed.com

"Music should feel like something happened.
Life."
